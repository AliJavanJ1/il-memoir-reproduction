FB pretraining: task-blind replay transitions, FB updates and their wall time, shared by the goals of a layout and seed; one-task total = pretraining + adaptation; amortized = pretraining / (number of goals sharing it) + adaptation. f-PG: its own learner transitions and wall time. Adaptation collects no transition. Evaluation is reported separately and never counts as training data; one evaluation job covers both ER_FB and DM_FB checkpoints of an adaptation run, so they share its evaluation cost. ## Notes on what these numbers do and do not show

- State occupancy matching does not identify the expert's action choices; the finite js_sa column is a diagnostic, and return and success are evaluation-only quantities that no learner optimizes.
- f-PG optimizes a smoothed plug-in objective against the 4-demonstration target; its gradient estimate is biased (theory FPG-5(b), FPG-9). DM_FB minimizes the model's estimate of its objective; nothing certifies the true objective (FBIL-2(e)). No convergence or expert-recovery guarantee applies to these runs.
- Three seeds do not support broad superiority claims; read the individual seeds.
- Continuous JS values are plug-in estimates from finite samples (biased upward); compare them with the expert row of the same goal.
